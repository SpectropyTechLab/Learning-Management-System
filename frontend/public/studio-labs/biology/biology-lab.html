Chart · Photosynthesis
6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ in sunlight, with chlorophyll
Raw materialsCO₂ (via stomata), water (via roots & xylem)
ProductsGlucose (stored as starch), oxygen
ConditionsSunlight, chlorophyll
SiteChloroplasts, mainly in palisade cells
1 · Light reaction (grana)Chlorophyll absorbs light; water is split (photolysis), releasing O₂; ATP and NADPH are made.
2 · Dark reaction / Calvin cycle (stroma)CO₂ is fixed into glucose using ATP and NADPH.
Try this 1. Move the lamp closer — the rate rises, then levels off. 2. Add more NaHCO₃ — the plateau rises (CO₂ was limiting). 3. Heat above 35 °C — enzymes denature and the rate falls. 4. Use a green filter — chlorophyll reflects green light.
