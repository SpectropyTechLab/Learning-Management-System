# Subject-specific visual directions

Choose one direction that supports the topic. Vary it for later outputs; the LMS must not look like one repeated template.

## Physics

- Precision laboratory: warm ivory or graphite instrument field, steel apparatus, red/blue vectors, amber measurements.
- Cosmic field: midnight gradient impression using layered dark solids, electric blue field lines, magenta sources, white trajectories.
- Technical blueprint: desaturated blue field, white construction lines, orange moving bodies, mint results.
- Real-world stage: simplified classroom/road/machine context with sky, ground, apparatus and high-contrast overlays.

## Chemistry

- Glass laboratory: pale cool background, translucent-looking vessels built from layered primitives, reagent colours, dark labels.
- Molecular theatre: rich plum/indigo field, luminous atoms, bond geometry and energetic collision flashes.
- Reaction pathway: warm charcoal or parchment field, clear reactant/product zones, energy profile and progress staging.
- Periodic/material explorer: light neutral field, categorical colours, crystalline or lattice arrangements and comparison callouts.

## Biology

- Microscopy: deep teal field, softly layered tissue/cell forms, coral and lime organelles, membrane depth.
- Medical atlas: warm off-white field, anatomical reds/blues, precise labels and directional flow arrows.
- Ecosystem: natural sky/soil/water layers, organic greens and earth tones, cyclic movement and energy flow.
- Molecular biology: dark violet field, DNA/protein colour coding, sequence direction, binding and transport motion.

## Mathematics

- Clean graph paper: warm white field, subtle grid, cobalt curve, vermilion points, dark labels.
- Geometric construction: deep slate field, gold construction lines, cyan loci, proof markers and symmetry.
- Data studio: light neutral cards represented within the scene, contrasting series colours and direct labels.
- Transformation theatre: dark or gradient-impression field, original/transformed figures, motion paths and before/after states.

## Variation rules

- Avoid the same dominant background and accent pair as the most recently supplied example.
- The topic decides the metaphor: apparatus, molecular chamber, cell/tissue, coordinate plane, real-world context, or process timeline.
- Light themes are allowed and encouraged when readable. `scene.background` and every label/line must maintain strong contrast.
- Use colour semantically and consistently; never change scientific category colours merely for decoration.
- A scene must include foreground emphasis, the core explanatory model, and background/context detail.
